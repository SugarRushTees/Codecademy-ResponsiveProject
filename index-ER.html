Beginnings: The start of my love for 2 wheels

Over a decade ago, going to university in Auckland meant getting up at 7am to walk steep hills in the rain and then spend over an hour sitting or standing on a crowded bus, just to walk up more steep hills in the rain to get to class. And that was just one way... The solution? Despite scaring the hell out of me, I started off on a 150cc Vespa, and worked my way up to my very first motorbike. Outcome? Staying home until 8am, whizzing in about 1/3 of the time, no walking, no wet socks, no sweaty brow, and best of all..

Monthly Motosocial

On the first Tuesday of every month, volunteers organise a social gathering at a secret location, where bikies travel far and wide to come show of their bikes, talk sh*t and organise social rides.
